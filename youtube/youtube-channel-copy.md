2. **PR Explained: India Edition**: Plain-language explainers on how PR works in India: press releases, media relations, crisis communication, personal branding and how to measure PR.
3. **Ad of the Week**: A weekly breakdown of a notable Indian or global ad: the idea, the craft, the strategy and what any brand can learn from it.
4. **Influencer & Digital Playbook**: Practical guides to influencer marketing, social media strategy, content calendars and digital campaigns, including compliance basics such as ad disclosures.
5. **Brand Films & Behind the Scenes**: How brand films are made, from concept and script to shoot and edit, plus behind-the-scenes looks at the agency's own creative work (shared with permission).

## YouTube Studio setup checklist
1. Create the channel. Signed in with the agency Google account, choose **Create a channel** and use the name **Lumiere Veritas | Media & PR**. It's best to create it as a brand account, so that other people can be given access later.
2. Open YouTube Studio (studio.youtube.com) and go to **Customisation** in the left menu.
3. **Customisation → Branding**:
   - **Picture**: upload `yt-profile-800x800.jpg`.
   - **Banner image**: upload `yt-banner-2560x1440.jpg`. Check the device previews; all the key content sits inside the safe area.
   - **Video watermark**: upload `yt-watermark-150x150.png`. Set it to show at the end of the video or for the entire video.
   - Click **Publish**.
4. **Customisation → Basic info**:
   - **Name**: Lumiere Veritas | Media & PR.
   - **Handle**: try @lumiereveritas, or @lumiereveritasmedia if it's taken.
   - **Description**: paste the channel description above.
   - **Links**: add Website and Instagram.
   - **Contact info**: add lumiereveritasmediasolutions@gmail.com. Leave out any phone number.
   - Click **Publish**.
5. **Settings → Channel → Basic info**: paste the channel keywords and set the country to India. Then **Save**.
6. **Settings → Upload defaults**: paste the upload description template and set default tags. Then **Save**.
7. **Create → New playlist** (or **Content → Playlists**): create the 5 playlists above with their descriptions.
8. Upload the trailer. Under **Video elements → End screen**, add the subscribe element and 2 video elements over the marked spaces on `yt-trailer-endscreen-1920x1080.jpg`.
9. **Customisation → Layout**: set the trailer as the **channel trailer for people who haven't subscribed**, then add featured sections, such as your playlists, and **Publish**.
10. For each video, use a thumbnail from `make_thumbs.py`: edit the text, then run `python3 make_thumbs.py`. Custom thumbnails may require phone verification of your account, which is done privately in Studio and doesn't publish the number.
